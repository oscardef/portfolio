## Overview

The site you're looking at. Instead of separate pages for experience and projects, it tells the story by place: every city I've lived in, in order, with what I did there attached to it.

## How It Works

- **The globe** is drawn on a canvas with d3-geo's orthographic projection and Natural Earth country shapes. Each leg of the route is a great-circle arc lifted off the surface; the camera eases between places and zooms in on Europe, where the stops are close together.
- **Scrolling drives it.** Each chapter reports when it crosses the middle of the screen, and the globe flies there. Side trips, like a summer in Abu Dhabi during my Groningen years, get their own detour when you reach them.
- **Performance:** the globe's code and map data load only as you approach the section, it stops drawing when off screen, and it holds still for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
- **Sheets:** projects and roles open over the page through Next.js intercepting routes. The URL changes, the page keeps its place, and the same URL renders a full page when visited directly.
- **Content** lives in MDX files with frontmatter validated by Zod at build time; a `chapter` field attaches each project or role to a place.

## Stack

- **Next.js 16** with the App Router and static generation, on React 19
- **TypeScript** and **Tailwind CSS 4**
- **MDX** via next-mdx-remote, with Zod for frontmatter
- **d3-geo** and **world-atlas** for the globe
- **Vercel** for hosting
